In the trade since 2003

The stitch came first.

Patchsmith has been in the trade since 2003. Two decades of craft, spent on one problem: turning a logo into a mark someone will actually wear. The workshop began with thread, and the stitch is still the standard the rest of the work is judged by.

That standard is particular. Stitch density, the edge, the colour, and the way a patch sits on a cap after a season of wear. A finished logo, a rough file or a sketch on paper all come through the same door. We draw a plan from it, and that plan is what you approve before anything is made.

The briefs come from brands, teams, clubs and businesses. Apparel labels, uniform suppliers, sports clubs, schools, events. We would rather work as a partner on the order than hand over a menu. If the lettering is too fine for embroidery, we say so. If woven, PVC, chenille or leather will read more clearly, the mockup is made that way instead.

Patches stay at the centre. Keychains, enamel pins, stickers and medals are there when the same mark needs to leave the garment. The aim is not a longer catalogue. It is a workshop that takes the logo seriously, agrees the finish with you, and makes what was approved.

Backings

How it stays on.

Iron-on
A heat-activated film. Press it onto cotton or canvas. Skip the iron on leather garments. The heat is wrong for them.
Sew-on
Stitched in place. The hold for jackets, uniforms and anything that gets washed hard.
Hook-and-loop
Velcro, so the patch can come off and go back. The usual pair with PVC.
Adhesive
Peel-and-stick, for a surface you will not sew. Tell us what it is going on.
Pin
A pin back when the same patch needs to move between garments.

Borders

How the edge finishes.

Merrowed
A wrapped overlock edge. The usual finish on an embroidered patch, and it keeps the rim from fraying.
Hot-cut
A heat-sealed edge. Clean on woven patches and on embroidered shapes that should sit flat.
Laser-cut
A precise cut when the outline should follow the logo, not a rectangle.

The five finishes

Which patch, and why.

Send the logo and what it is going on. One finish gets recommended, then mocked up, before anything is made.

  1. 01

    Embroidered

    Thread stitched through a twill ground, so the logo sits raised. This is the finish most caps, jackets and uniforms are asking for. A name, a crest or a short wordmark belongs here.

    Your artwork becomes a stitch plan, and that plan is the mockup you approve. If the lettering is tiny, we will usually move it to woven and say why.

  2. 02

    Woven

    A finer thread in a flat weave. Small type and thin lines stay sharp, and the patch sits thinner than embroidery, which helps on pockets, collars and labels.

    Send the logo as it is. If the detail is too fine for a stitch, the mockup comes back woven.

  3. 03

    PVC

    A soft patch moulded in flexible rubber. It holds shape and colour in the wet, which is why it lives on bags and outdoor kit. The outline can follow the logo.

    Hook-and-loop lets it come off. Iron-on, sew-on or adhesive when it should stay. The backing is matched to the material on the quote.

  4. 04

    Chenille

    Looped yarn on a felt ground. The letter stands up and feels soft. It is the varsity finish: school letters, club numbers, retro merch on jackets and hoodies.

    Bold shapes read best. Fine lines stay on embroidery or woven, and the mockup will show which.

  5. 05

    Leather

    Leather or faux leather, debossed or printed. A quieter mark than bright thread, for hats, denim and bags.

    On a leather garment, sew-on or adhesive is the safer fix. A hot iron is a poor match for leather.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

The short version of how an order is priced, timed and shipped. Pricing depends on patch type, size and quantity. Send your design for a quote.

How does pricing work?

Pricing depends on patch type, size and quantity. Send your design for a quote. Each order is quoted on its own. There is no price list on this page.

How long does it take?

It depends on the finish, the size and the quantity. The timing comes with the quote, and production starts after you approve the mockup.

Do you ship to my country?

Ships worldwide by tracked express courier.

What file should I send?

Vector (AI, EPS, PDF, SVG) is ideal. A clear PNG, JPG, or a photo of a sketch is enough to start the mockup.

Can I see it before you make it?

Yes. Free digital mockup with every quote. Production starts only after you approve it.

Which patch type should I choose?

Embroidery for a raised logo, woven for small type, PVC for outdoor kit, chenille for varsity letters, leather for a quieter mark. See patch types, or send the artwork and what it is going on and we'll recommend one.

Can you match my brand colours?

Send your Pantone references and we'll match thread colours as closely as the material allows.

How do I pay?

How you pay is confirmed in writing when the order is agreed. Nothing is made before the mockup is approved.

Do you do samples?

A pre-production sample is something to ask about with the quote. We'll say what is possible for that design.

Which backing should I pick?

Iron-on for cotton and canvas. Sew-on when it has to survive washing. Hook-and-loop when it should come off, which is the usual PVC choice. Adhesive or a pin when you won't sew it. On leather garments, skip the iron.

Merrowed, hot-cut or laser-cut?

Merrowed is the wrapped edge on most embroidered patches. Hot-cut seals a woven or flat edge. Laser-cut follows a custom outline. We'll match the edge to the finish in the mockup.

Do you make keychains, pins, stickers or medals?

Yes. Patches are the main offer. Keychains, enamel pins, stickers and medals sit alongside them. Same path: artwork, a quote, a free digital mockup, then production after you approve. See the extra lines.
