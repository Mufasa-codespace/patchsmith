Five finishes

Pick your patch.

We'll handle the rest. Not sure which finish fits? Send the artwork and what it's going on. We'll tell you.

Embroidered

The classic. Raised, textured thread for logos that want to be felt. Best for caps, jackets, uniforms.

Woven

Thinner thread, sharper detail. If your logo has small text or fine lines, this is your patch.

PVC

Soft rubber, fully waterproof, practically indestructible. Made for outdoor gear, tactical kit and bags.

Chenille

Fuzzy, bold, unmistakably varsity. Letterman jackets, school spirit, retro merch.

Leather

Debossed or printed, leather or faux leather, for a premium feel. Denim, hats, bags, high-end labels.

Backings iron-on · sew-on · hook-and-loop (Velcro) · adhesive · pin

Borders merrowed · hot-cut · laser-cut
